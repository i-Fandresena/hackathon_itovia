# Evidence market — Madagascar

Use these sources for bounded claims. Refresh the source before a public pitch
if it is older than six months or if the number is material to the argument.

| Observation | Use it to argue | Source and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6.60 M internet users and 18.2 M mobile connections in January 2025; internet penetration 20.4%. | Design mobile-first, data-light and assisted onboarding. Do not claim nationwide digital reach. | [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-madagascar), compiled market data. |
| Adult informal employment was 94.4% in 2022; youth informal employment 98.9%. | The issue is job quality, trust and market access. | [World Bank](https://humancapital.worldbank.org/en/economy/MDG). Keep indicator definitions with the claim. |
| AfDB reports informality at 83.3% of the economy and 95.2% of employment, citing 2020 estimates. | A verified reputation can help informal micro-enterprises gain demand and progress toward formalisation. | [AfDB CSP review](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/madagascar_-_mid-term_review_of_the_country_strategy_paper_2022-2026.pdf). Macro estimate, not an OffRec market-size figure. |
| ILO's ProAgro Youth targeted 1,500 rural youth for decent work/self-employment in Madagascar. | Employment and entrepreneurship are active development priorities. | [ILO project](https://www.ilo.org/fr/projects-and-partnerships/projects/accroitre-le-nombre-demplois-salaries-et-auto-emplois-decents-pour-les). |
| Asako, Jobmada and Asa Mada already publish local job offers. | Do not compete on job-listing volume; differentiate via verified local service reputation and outcomes. | [Asako](https://www.asako.mg/), [Jobmada](https://www.jobmada.mg/), [Asa Mada](https://www.asamada.com/). Features change frequently. |

## Claim discipline

- Say « marché initial accessible » rather than TAM without a documented sizing method.
- Say « hypothèse de prix » rather than revenue forecast until paid pilots.
- Never use competitor marketing or anecdotes as quantitative evidence.
